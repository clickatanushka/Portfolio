About Me

Building products that combine design, engineering & electronics manufacturing

With my experience in electronics manufacturing, PCB management, and production and project coordination, I have supported technical products from the initial cost estimation through to successful production.

My strength lies in connecting technical requirements, economic goals, and production processes. I work in a structured, solution-oriented manner with a strong focus on quality — whether it is BOM management, cost calculation, supplier coordination, production planning, or customer communication.

In a dynamic industrial environment, I see myself as a link between engineering, procurement, production, suppliers, and customers. My goal is to improve processes, identify challenges early, and successfully bring projects to completion.

I am convinced that sustainable success is achieved not only through technical know-how, but also through responsibility, teamwork, and open communication. With international experience and a strong quality mindset, I support companies in efficiently bringing innovative products into production.
